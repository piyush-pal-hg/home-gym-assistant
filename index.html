  • Bicycle crunch 20
  • Leg raise 12
  • Plank 30–45 sec

Shoulders + chest (PM, 4 rounds)

  • Pike push-up 8–12
  • Push-up 10–20
  • Wide push-up 10
  • Plank shoulder tap 20

Tips for a slim, broad-shoulder look

  • Broad shoulders: pike push-ups, wide push-ups and shoulder taps build the shoulder caps. Slim waist plus wider shoulders gives the V-shape.
  • Visible abs: abs show when body fat is low. Train core 4 times a week, but fix food first.
  • Food: protein at every meal, lots of water, fewer sugary drinks and fried snacks. Don’t starve.
  • Sleep: 7–8 hours. Poor sleep adds belly and face puffiness.
  • Posture: shoulders back, chin slightly tucked, while sitting in college and travelling.
  • Be patient: real change takes 8–12 weeks of consistency. Take a front and side photo every 2 weeks.
